Official Privacy Policy

Room Temperature Thermometer

Last Updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Learning Easy ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use the Room Temperature Thermometer mobile application (Package Name: com.smartroom.temperature.thermometer). We are committed to transparency and upholding your data privacy rights.

Key Privacy Guarantee: Indoor temperature calculations (hardware sensor telemetry and thermodynamic battery models) are executed 100% locally on your device. We never transmit or store your indoor ambient readings on external servers.

1. Information We Collect and Process

A. Location Information

When you use the outdoor weather, hourly forecast, and Air Quality Index (AQI) features, the application requests access to your device's location:

  • Approximate Location (Coarse Location): Used to determine your city or geographical region to fetch current weather and air pollutant metrics.
  • Precise Location (Fine Location): Used solely to link to the nearest meteorological observation station for hyper-local telemetry.

Note: Location data is transmitted directly to external weather data APIs over secure encrypted connections (HTTPS) and is never sold, logged, or shared for third-party tracking.

B. Technical and Device Information

To ensure application stability and proper functionality, third-party libraries integrated into our app may automatically collect certain diagnostic and non-personally identifiable technical information, including:

  • Device manufacturer, model, and Android OS version.
  • Google Advertising ID (GAID) for advertising and attribution.
  • Crash logs, stack traces, and app performance metrics.

2. Third-Party Services & SDK Disclosures

Our app incorporates trusted third-party services to deliver advertising, analytics, and billing infrastructure. Each partner operates under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Used to display banner, native, and interstitial advertisements. Complies with Google Play Families policies and supports user consent choices via the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google Ads Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing: Facilitates secure, encrypted in-app purchases and subscriptions for Pro access. All financial transactions are handled directly by Google Play. We do not store or process payment card details. Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics: Collects anonymized crash diagnostics and engagement patterns to improve app stability. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Sends weather alert notifications and periodic service updates to your device token.

3. Device Permissions & Transparency

We request only the minimum Android runtime permissions essential for app operation:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Required to obtain outdoor temperature, atmospheric pressure, humidity, and AQI from local weather stations.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Required if you opt in to receive daily climate forecasts and severe weather advisories.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to fetch outdoor weather metrics and verify network availability.
  • BILLING: Required to manage and restore your optional Pro subscriptions or lifetime ad-free purchase.

4. User Rights & Data Deletion Mechanism

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy regulations (including GDPR and CCPA), you have full control over your data:

  • You can revoke location or notification permissions at any time via your device's Settings > Apps > Room Temperature > Permissions.
  • You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising via your device's Settings > Google > Ads.
  • To request the deletion of any diagnostic data or inquire about your data records, please submit a request to our support email at learningeasyapps@gmail.com. We will process your request within 30 business days.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is not targeted toward children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security protocols and HTTPS encryption for all external communications to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
